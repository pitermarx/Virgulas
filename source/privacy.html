Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026.

Virgulas is local-first. In Memory, Local, and File modes nothing you write is ever sent to a Virgulas server. Remote mode stores a single encrypted document so you can sync between devices; the server never receives the key.

What is stored on this device

Everything below lives in your browser and never leaves it except as noted in the next section. Only the encrypted document is secret; the rest is non-secret operational data, and every item can be removed from your device at any time.

What reaches our servers

  • Remote mode only — your account email and password go to Supabase Auth, and the encrypted document plus its random salt are stored in our Supabase-hosted database, keyed to your account. Your encryption passphrase is never sent to any server.
  • Delivery and security — the site is served through Cloudflare's network, and the database is hosted by Supabase (Supabase Inc.). Their infrastructure processes IP addresses, as any web server does, for delivery, security and abuse prevention.
  • Analytics — we self-host an Umami instance at um.vps.pitermarx.com on our own server. It is cookieless, collects page views (path, referrer, browser/OS, screen size), and derives an approximate country from your IP address. It never receives URL query strings or fragments (so quick-capture links and shared node links are not sent), does not build a profile or track you across sites, and is disabled when you enable the Do Not Track signal in your browser.

What we cannot see

The document is encrypted on your device with AES-GCM-256 using a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). The server stores only ciphertext and cannot read it — in any storage mode. If you forget your passphrase, the data cannot be recovered — not even by us.

Deleting your data

On the device, Options → Delete local data removes the encrypted outline, the quick-capture queue, the remembered email and session, the biometric seal, and the offline caches. In Remote mode, Options → Delete account removes the encrypted row from our database immediately and signs you out. To have the account record itself erased as well, or to exercise access, rectification, portability, or objection rights, contact us through the repository link below.

Legal basis, and retention

Where the GDPR applies, we process your account email to perform the contract (Remote mode) and aggregate analytics on the basis of legitimate interest. Your account email and the encrypted document are kept only while your account exists; Supabase Auth keeps short-lived authentication logs for security. We do not sell or share personal data.

Global Privacy Control. We do not sell or share personal data, so a GPC signal requires no change in behaviour. We advertise this at /.well-known/gpc.json.
